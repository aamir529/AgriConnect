RURAL INCLUSION INNOVATION

Designed for Bharat: Zero Smartphone Literacy Barrier

India's marginal farmers don't need expensive smartphones or complicated apps. AgriConnect's Assisted-Digital architecture bridges the digital divide through local village youth facilitators (VDFs) and toll-free IVR voice dialing.

Toll-Free IVR Hotline

Farmers dial 1800-889-SETU from any basic feature phone to record produce listings in Hindi.

Direct SMS Bank Slips

Instant SMS confirmation sent to farmer's mobile upon listing, grading, and direct DBT bank settlement.

Role of Village Digital Facilitator (VDF)
  • Aggregates produce from 30–50 surrounding farmers
  • Digital weighing & quality grading at Village CSC Hub
  • Earns transparent 5% facilitation income per batch
  • Verifies automated direct payments into farmer bank accounts
